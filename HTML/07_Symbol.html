Symbols in HTML

HTML provides a variety of symbols that can be used to represent special characters, mathematical symbols, currency symbols, and more. Here are some examples:

  • & - Ampersand
  • < - Less than
  • > - Greater than
  • © - Copyright
  • ® - Registered Trademark
  • € - Euro Currency Symbol
  • £ - Pound Sterling Symbol
  • ¥ - Yen Symbol
  • ★ - Star Symbol
