HTML Entities

HTML entities are special characters that are used to represent reserved characters in HTML. They are used to display characters that have a special meaning in HTML, such as <, >, &, and ".

Common HTML Entities

& amp; (without spaces)
Represents the ampersand character (&).
& lt; (without spaces)
Represents the less-than sign (<).
& gt; (without spaces)
Represents the greater-than sign (>).)
& quot; (without spaces)
Represents the double quotation mark (").
& apos; (without spaces)
Represents the single quotation mark (').
& copy; (without spaces)
Represents the copyright symbol (©).
& reg; (without spaces)
Represents the registered trademark symbol (®).
& trade; (without spaces)
Represents the trademark symbol (™).

Non-Breaking Spaces

Non-breaking spaces are used to create extra spaces in HTML. They are represented by the entity   (without spaces). For example:

Combining Diacritical Marks

Combining diacritical marks are used to add accents and other symbols to characters. They are represented by Unicode characters. For example:

café( a& #768;)
