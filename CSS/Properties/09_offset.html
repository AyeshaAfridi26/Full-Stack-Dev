Position Offsets — top, right, bottom, left

Once you set position to anything other than static, you can use four offset properties to move the element: top, right, bottom, left.

How Offsets Work

Normal position
top: 20px
(moves DOWN 20px from top edge)
left: 30px
(moves RIGHT 30px from left edge)
top: -15px
left: -15px
(negative = moves UP and LEFT)

Z-Index — What Appears on Top

When elements overlap, z-index decides which one appears on top. Higher z-index = closer to you (in front). Lower z-index = further from you (behind).

Think of z-index like layers in Photoshop — or a stack of cards. The card on top has the highest z-index.

Important Rule

position: relative
z-index: 10
Works! z-index only works on positioned elements (relative, absolute, fixed, sticky)
position: static
z-index: 10
Does nothing! z-index is ignored on position: static (the default)
